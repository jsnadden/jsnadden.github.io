About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Perth, WA, with a PhD in mathematics from Northwestern University. My research was primarily in mathematical physics, specifically in developing concrete computational tools to solve problems arising in graph theory, low-dimensional topology, statistical physics, and string theory.

I previously worked at the Pawsey Centre and CSIRO, where I helped to develop and run highly-parallel simulations of finite-element multi-physics models. Since completing my PhD, I've returned to the world of software development, with an enduring interest in low-level systems. I've worked on a number of personal projects, most notably my from-scratch game engine Zahra.

I'm drawn to work that requires both mathematical reasoning and careful engineering: simulation, scientific computing, and any domain where getting the fundamentals right actually matters.
